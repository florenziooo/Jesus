### Church Attendance  *"In the temple..."*

>"And let us not neglect our meeting together, as some people do, but encourage one another, especially now that the day of His return is drawing near." - Hebrews 10:25

The church is a group of people who love God.

### Cell  *"from house to house..."*

A cell is where you can receive encouragement, acceptance, and love.
It meets in houses or public places for you to fellowship with other Christians in a small group setting.
Intimate.
Doing life—not just church.


### Equipping   *they continued to teach...*

God would like to use you to fulfill his purpose for your life.
Everyone has the potential to become a leader (Genesis 1:26).
You need to be trained and equipped to become effective at what God wants us to do.

Someone was changed by God in order to meet you.
He is so grateful to be a witness for your growth.
God wants you to use you to change many lives.

There are lives clinging to your 'yes'.
It has to start with you.

### Evangelism   *and preach this message...*
You need to tell others about Jesus.
Tell everybody that you have already accepted Him as your personal Lord and Savior.
Preaching the gospel is God's great commission that needs to be obeyed.


>"Go into all the world and preach the Good News to everyone. Anyone who believes and is baptized will be saved. But anyone who refuses to believe will be condemned" - Mark 16:15-16

## What the Lord is impressing me about you
You are called to be a leader.
You are a gifted at discerning.
- Keen observer
- You are able to spot things most people don't see.
- You recognize specific patterns of insight, empathy, and spiritual sensitivity in your daily life.
- You can sense easily the atmospheres in the room, building, or gathering.
- You have a slow but steady pursuit of truth.

If you have close proximity to the Lord's voice, He will teach you:
- Learn to discern revelation from God or not from God.
- Learn to discern deception from truth.
- Learn to discern if someone is fruitful for what they believe in.
- Learn to diagnose what this person is going through, and helping them break out of it.
- Learn if that person—or even yourself—has the fruit of the Holy Spirt. (to have that transformation evident)
- Learn to claim the vision that you see for yourself AND for other people. And you have the authority to do so because you are a son of the living God.

And those can be learned with the teachings of His word, and a little exposure in life—that means that the enemy will challenge you in his different schemes to stumble you and discourage you.
And I want you to be aware of it.

If you stay close to His voice, much more things will be unlocked for you.
You bring more impact for a lot of people more than what you can imagine for yourself.
Not just in this lifetime, but in eternity as well.
Not just in breadth, but in depth.

As faithful disciples, you must recognize that every problem that you might encounter in your life, is an opportunity to be strengthen to hold a lot of weight.
And being strengthened means to have our roots deepened down; to be rooted firmly and deeply to the word of God.
