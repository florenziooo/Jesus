## 1. God's Original Design

Before we talk about sin, we have to understand what God intended.

When God created Adam and Eve (Genesis 1–2), everything was good. They were made in His image and placed in the Garden of Eden, not merely a beautiful place, but a place where they *enjoyed His presence*.

**We were designed to live in relationship with God.**

> "He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also set eternity in the human heart…"
> — [[Verse Bank#^ecclesiastes-3-11|Ecclesiastes 3:11]]

God placed eternity in every human heart. That is why no success, money, relationship, achievement, or pleasure can fully satisfy us. We were created for something greater. We were created for God.

> [!question] Ask
> "Have you ever felt like something was missing, even when life seemed okay?"

That longing points back to our original design.

---

## 2. What Happened?

God gave Adam and Eve one command.

> "…but you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, for when you eat from it you will certainly die."
> — [[Verse Bank#^genesis-2-17|Genesis 2:17]]

> [!question] Ask
> "Did Adam and Eve physically die that day?"

No. But *something* died:

- Their intimacy with God
- Their innocence
- Their peace
- Their identity

Physical death followed later.

---

## 3. What Is Sin?

Many people think sin is simply breaking God's rules. It is deeper than that.

**Sin is choosing our own way instead of God's.**
It is stepping outside the life we were created for.

Imagine taking a fish out of water. Did the fish suddenly become bad? No. It just wasn't designed to live outside the water.

In the same way, **humanity wasn't designed to live apart from God.** When we leave His design, things begin to die: relationships, purpose, peace, hope, joy. That is why we experience guilt, shame, fear, anxiety, emptiness, and brokenness.

> "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."
> — [[Verse Bank#^romans-3-23|Romans 3:23]]

No one is exempt. Every one of us has stepped outside God's design.

> [!question] Ask
> "Can you think of a time when sin left you feeling empty, guilty, ashamed, or broken?"

---

## 4. Why Sin Is Serious

God is perfectly holy, perfectly just, and perfectly faithful. Everything He says is *true*. When He said sin leads to death, that was not an empty threat. God cannot lie, and He cannot ignore sin.

> [!question] Ask
> "Have you sinned?" … Then: "Did you physically die immediately?"

No. But something always dies: peace, joy, relationships, trust, purpose. And the greatest consequence is separation from God.

> "For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."
> — [[Verse Bank#^romans-6-23|Romans 6:23]]

Sin earns death, not only physical death, but **spiritual death: separation from God.**

---

## 5. Two Destinations

Jesus spoke about both heaven and hell because He loves us enough to warn us.

- **Heaven** is eternal life in the presence of God.
- **Hell** is eternal separation from God.

> [!question] Ask
> "When you think of hell, what picture comes to mind?"

Many picture boiling lava and physical torture. But think of every good thing you have ever experienced: love, peace, joy, hope. Those are reflections of God's goodness. Now imagine complete separation from all of it, forever.

**But God…**

> "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."
> — [[Verse Bank#^john-3-16|John 3:16]]

Jesus came so that no one would have to face that separation.

---

## 6. God Already Had a Plan

Immediately after humanity sinned, God did not abandon them. He made a promise.

> "And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and hers; he will crush your head, and you will strike his heel."
> — [[Verse Bank#^genesis-3-15|Genesis 3:15]]

This is often called the *first gospel*. It was as if God was saying:

- *"I Myself will deal with sin."*
- *"I Myself will rescue humanity."*
- *"I Myself will make a way."*

## 7. Why Did Jesus Have to Die?

When sin first entered heaven, God could have destroyed Satan in an instant. He had the power, the authority, and every reason.

**So why didn't He?**
Why allow thousands of years of suffering, pain, and death?
Why did Jesus have to die instead?

The answer is found in God's character.

> "Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love."
> — [[Verse Bank#^1-john-4-8|1 John 4:8]]

Love cannot be proven by force. It must be revealed.

If God had destroyed Satan immediately, the universe would have obeyed out of fear rather than love, and the questions would have remained:

- *"Was Satan right?"*
- *"Is God unfair?"*
- *"Does God silence anyone who disagrees with Him?"*

The controversy would have ended by power, not by truth. So God allowed sin to reveal its true nature. Every tear. Every war. Every broken home. Every grave. **Sin testified against itself.**

### Calvary

When Jesus hung on the cross, Satan thought he had won, but the cross exposed him completely.

- The One who healed the sick was crucified.
- The One who forgave sinners was condemned.
- The One who had no sin died for those who did.

At the cross, Satan revealed the depth of his hatred.
At the cross, God revealed the depth of His love.

> "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."
> — [[Verse Bank#^romans-5-8|Romans 5:8]]

The victory did not come because God out-powered Satan. Everyone already knew God was more powerful. **The victory came because God answered hatred with sacrificial love.**

> "It is finished."
> — [[Verse Bank#^john-19-30|John 19:30]]

The issue was never whether God was the strongest. The issue was whether God was **worthy of our trust**. The cross answered that forever.

### Our Share in the Victory

> "They triumphed over him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony."
> — [[Verse Bank#^revelation-12-11|Revelation 12:11]]

We do not overcome by our own strength. We overcome because Christ already won.

One day Satan will be destroyed forever ([[Verse Bank#^revelation-20-10|Revelation 20:10]]). When that day comes, the whole universe will know that God's judgment is not the act of a cruel ruler. It is the righteous act of a loving King who gave every opportunity for repentance and proved His character beyond all doubt.

> God could have destroyed Satan at the beginning with His power.
> Instead, He defeated him through the cross with His love.
>
> **Power can force obedience, but only love can win a heart.**

## 8. What Happened on the Cross?

Read **Isaiah 53** together.

> "But he was pierced for our transgressions, he was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was on him, and by his wounds we are healed. We all, like sheep, have gone astray, each of us has turned to our own way; and the LORD has laid on him the iniquity of us all."
> — [[Verse Bank#^isaiah-53-5-6|Isaiah 53:5–6]]

Jesus became unrecognizable. Not because of His own sin, but because He carried ours:

- Every lie
- Every pride
- Every addiction
- Every lust
- Every hatred
- Every selfish decision
- Every hidden sin

Everything was placed on Him.

This shows us two things:

1. **Sin is far more serious than we think.**
2. **God's love is far greater than we can imagine.**

The cross is where God's justice and God's love met.

---

## 9. Salvation Is a Gift

Many people think, *"If I become good enough, maybe God will accept me."* That is not the gospel.

> "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith—and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God—not by works, so that no one can boast."
> — [[Verse Bank#^ephesians-2-8-9|Ephesians 2:8–9]]

We cannot earn it. We cannot buy it. We cannot deserve it. **Jesus already paid for it.**

The only question is whether we will receive Him by faith.

- Share how you experienced His forgiveness, peace, purpose, and relationship.
- Point them to Jesus, not to yourself.
