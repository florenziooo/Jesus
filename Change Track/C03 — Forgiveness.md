[[Verse Bank#^matthew-18-21-35|Matthew 18:21–35]]

When you don’t forgive people, we develop a victim mentality, and develop an identity that keeps us safe from unforgiveness.

At the thought of forgiving that person, it almost feels like you’re losing control; feels like you’re losing a part of yourself.

What they did to you, and what you lost in the process, you’ll never get that back. Some may never have the capacity to come up to you and apologize.

What the Lord is saying is that, forgiveness is not for them. Forgiveness is for YOU TO BE FREE. It is to get out of the prison you have been in your soul. You’ve been in a prison for a long time.

No, they don’t deserve it. Neither did you.
This is the Lord saying “I care about your freedom so much I want you to live a life of boundless forgiveness. When people wound you, you need to let go. You’re not doing it for them. You’re doing it for you.” He want you to evict everybody in your heart that you’ve been carrying for a long time; a season you’re still stuck in.

Preach about the cross. Is the blood of Christ enough to pay for what they did?

It may take some time, but at least the decision you make to let them go starts the journey of HEALING.
