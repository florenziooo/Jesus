There are many differences between a person who is simply visiting a house, and the son or daughter in a home. Take some time to think about those differences and evaluate your own situation within the family of God. Do you feel like a child of God, or like a visitor?

A Son Vs. A Visitor

A son has access to every part of the house. A visitor, however, has limited access. A son can stay in his home as long as he wants, while a visitor is expected to leave the house after visiting. While the son has its privileges to his home, it also has responsibilities for taking care of the house. A visitor does not. A son loves his parents, while a visitor merely visits the house.

Until now I have felt like a son in the house of God because I was rescued from darkness—from further danger. He prepared me a comfortable place to live in. And like any other good parent, God only wants me to grow as a son and only wants the best for me.

In terms of my attitudes, I would like to change my stubborness and my irresponsibility so that I can be a better child of God.
