God has provision for every area of your life. Read through the following verses and connect each of God’s promises to the area they represent.

In [[Romans 8#^v38|Romans 8:38-39]], we are assured that our calling is secured because nothing can ever separate us from God’s love. If we are called to do according to His will, we can be sure that He will move immovable rocks for our sake.

In [[Psalm 2#^v8|Psalm 2:8]], we must partner with God to expand his heavenly kingdom, and he will give us inheritance to impact generations.

In [[Joshua 24#^v15|Joshua 24:15]], we must choose the LORD over any other idol. The verse reminded us that our work or studies are not random. They are a part of His design. So our part to do is to fulfill our careers that would not obstruct our devotion to the LORD.

In [[Jeremiah 29#^v11|Jeremiah 29:11]], In everything that I do, so long as to build for my future and my future children, I will be assured that God is with me. I will follow His vision he gave me; to walk by faith and not by sight.

In [[Psalm 20#^v4|Psalm 20:4]], I am sure that if he may not give what I’ve been praying—it’s because He knows that I need to be prepared for that prayer to come, and that takes time, effort, failures, disappointments. But I’m sure that He will never abandon me and He will make my plans succeed.
