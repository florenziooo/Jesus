Baptism is a transition between two states, a complete change, a total immersion. It is our opportunity to start over.

What habits or attitudes would you like to leave behind so that you can truly have a new beginning?

Forgetfulness, stubborness, brittle.

Pray and ask God to show you what other things are hindering you and not allowing you to have a new beginning?

Lord, search my soul. Show me my habits and other hidden parts of my heart that are hindering me to be close to You, Lord. I pray that you reveal things to me as You are eager in preparing for me.

Help me let go of my forgetfulness, stubborness, and being brittle. Help me in guarding my heart and being strong and steadfast in Your love.

Open my eyes if there are fears, wounds, or insecurities that I’ve overlooked. I trust You in whatever you are showing to me. I know that You can heal me Lord. In Jesus’ name, amen.
