Write down three characteristics of the worldly man and three characteristics of the spiritual man that are mentioned in [[Romans 8#^v5|Romans 8:5-11]].

Worldly man…
is controlled by flesh results in death,
becomes enemies of God,
does not obey with God’s law.

Spiritual man…
is controlled by the Spirit of Christ,
spirit will live—even his flesh will die.
will be given life and peace.
