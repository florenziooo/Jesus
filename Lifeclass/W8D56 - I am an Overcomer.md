Write a list of declarations of faith that you can read out loud every day.

I am loved unconditionally. Nothing can separate me from God’s love.
I will not lean into my own understanding. I trust God to set forth immovable rocks with His authority.

Though I am weak, but because of Jesus, God’s joy will always be my strength.
He will prune me; strengthen me—to be the best version of myself He designed me to be.

Through Jesus, I will be equipped to share everyone the Good News
That everyone must know that there is a Living God that never abandons.

This I declare—that I am a child of God
That nothing is impossible in His presence.
To be with Him is my inheritance.

I will be forever in debt of His grace.
I will surrender any burden that holds me back.

When I tasted His goodness,
I’d rather want what He calls me to.
I shall not want anything only for my sake.
I shall want no more.
