[[Matthew 14]]
Storms are not interruption.
Storms are spiritual classrooms.

Where is Jesus when storms are here?
Storms reveal capacity.
The deeper your roots, the stronger you go, the riper the fruit, the stabler the trunk.
God is not the problem, it’s the acknowledgement of capacity.

Fear distorts perception.
Doubt is the first step to faith.
Faith is stepping out of unfamiliar.
It’s okay if you encounter unknown as long as Jesus is with you. Because anxiety lets you think that you are in control, without knowing that Jesus is in control.
God allows you to drown, because He knows that you already near Him.
