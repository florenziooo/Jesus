Encounter is not the finish line. It is the beginning of your journey.
There are damages or marks made when you encounter.

The point of Encounter is to begin a life with God. Attached to that is to do life with us.

Now we walk in confidence—not arrogance. Because we have the chance to meet the Savior.

Don't live like prisoner when Jesus opened a door.

We must not submit momentarily to the things that Jesus has delivered you from.

Discipleship transitions a believer into a disciple.
Being delivered is one thing.
But staying delivered is done through discipleship.
Change environment.
Change habits.

God is concerned of your becoming than who you are now.

Embrace the pain of discipleship than the pain of regret.

The point of discipleship is to stay delivered.

God doesn't want to have a one night stand with you.
He wants to be engaged; committed with you.

Encounter gives you a moment.
Discipleship gives you a walk.
God-given destiny makes you desire to run.

We measure our encounter with God, not because of how emotional we are in the moment. But how much you are obeying Him.

Love God. Love people. Disciple them. Teach them to obey. All the things God has commanded.
