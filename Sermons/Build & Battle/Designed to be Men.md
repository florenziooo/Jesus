> [!quote] [[Genesis 3#^v8|Genesis 3:8-9]] ESV
> “And they heard the sound of the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool of the day, and the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the Lord God among the trees of the garden. But the Lord God called to the man and said to him, “Where are you?””

You are born a male, but does not automatically mean you’re a man. You can become an old male but not a man. To become a man, it requires an intentional decision.

“The Lord God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to work it and keep it.” [[Genesis 2#^v15|Genesis 2:15]] Man was given a responsibility—he was placed with a purpose.

> [!quote] [[Genesis 2#^v18|Genesis 2:18]]
> “Then the Lord God said, “It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him a helper fit for him.”

The assignment came first before the partner.

A woman is not given to create your purpose—she is given to help you fulfill it.
If you don’t understand your responsibility, if you’re not walking in your purpose, then you won’t fully understand the role of the person God brings into your life. Because how can someone help you build something… when you don’t yet know what you’re building?

But helper does not mean inferior.
The word “helper” here reflects strength, support, and counterpart partnership.

Fulfilling yourself to be a father is a spiritual thing. It is becoming one of God’s characteristics—being a father.
