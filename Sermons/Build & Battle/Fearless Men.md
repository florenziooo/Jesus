> [!info] Reference — [[Nehemiah 4#^v12|Nehemiah 4:12-17]]

Before the battle, remember the Lord.
Now when we step into faith, fear will be removed.

Before we lead families, children, marriages, we remember who led us from the start.

Learn to fight for what matters most. But the sad part is, we have been so excellent in pursuing, but we fail in stewarding.

Let’s fight for our becoming.

## Shrewd Disicples of Jesus

> [!info] Reference — [[Luke 16]]

Prepare for the future.
Plan ahead and identify temporary things.
Plan for the things that are eternal.
Steward what you have faithfully.
