Help me how to finish well. In order to finish well, we need the rhythm.

We need to tell the difference of resting from the ministry and resting for the ministry. So when we minister people, we are at our best condition.

We are stuck of being in—some of us are addicted to revelation but we don’t even release it.

We are stuck of being out—we love to preach and serve but we drain our resource. You have to change your appetite for the word.

What we confess and whar we profess are two different things.
When we do change track, the person is not talking to an individual. He is talking to the representative of the Kingdom of God.
Do we see like Jesus, think like Jesus and talk like Jesus.
We can do things like Jesus without the Spirit of Jesus.

Discipleship is never about following someone. It is about looking like Jesus.
