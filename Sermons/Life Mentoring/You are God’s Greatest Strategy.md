What is God’s greatest strategy to reach out to the lost? People.

- You are not here to attend Sundays.
- You are not here to support pastors and their visions.
- You are not here just to be changed weekly.
- You are here because God wants you to reach to the people that He loved.

Grow your people to grow the church.

## People-Oriented Growth

### Discipleship — this is how you grow your cell

Discipleship is not a program. It is a lifestyle. It’s part of your life. Don’t limit it to schedules—this is going to make it tedious.

→ [[Matthew 28#^v19|Matthew 28:19-20]]

### Dwelling of the Holy Spirit — you carry His presence

The Holy Spirit dwells on people, not programs.

→ [[1 Corinthians 6#^v19|1 Corinthians 6:19]]

### Deep Relationships — you connect

People connect to people. Surround yourself with people with the right heart and the right spirit.

→ [[John 13#^v34|John 13:34-35]]

### Development — you grow

We don’t use people to run programs. We use programs to develop people.

→ [[Ephesians 4#^v11|Ephesians 4:11-12]]

### Deployment — you are sent

People-centered growth doesn’t keep ministry around the pastor. It release people. “I would never follow a leader who didn’t suffer.”

### Duplication — you reproduce

A disciple should eventually produce another disciple.

→ [[2 Timothy 2#^v2|2 Timothy 2:2]]

### Dispersion — take the mission everywhere

A program has a location. A discople has a mission everywhere.

→ [[Acts 1#^v8|Acts 1:8]]

## The Result

- If you develop discoples, the church becomes stronger.
- If you develop leaders, the church becomes bigger.
- If you develop disciple-makers, the church becomes multiplied. That is people oriented-growth.

Prayer is not the preparation for the work. Prayer IS the work.

## Common Struggles

- I’m afraid to because I don’t know how.
- I’m afraid of being rejected, maybe I get debated.
- Why do I share it? It’s not my job.

### Answer

- Go and tell you story.
- People love to hear stories
- You don’t need a program. It’s your story.
- Story is relatable.
