Maturity stops when expectations were unmet.

Formal ideologies that mismatch to the true revelation of Jesus.

The root of these is all pride.
The antidote of immaturity is humility.
Childlike faith is not childish.

If a child knows his father, he should understand why things can’t be acquired now. But he knows that his father will show up and give you in ways you can’t even imagine.

Only teachable are entrusted.
You can’t grow maturity if you can’t have childlike faith.
