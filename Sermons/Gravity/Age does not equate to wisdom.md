[[Job 1|JOB 1]], 32.

Your rightneousness does not exempt you from suffering.
We’re entitled to think how God will move.
Sometimes God’s approval comes in a form of a test.
Will Job remain faithful when this walk becomes costly?
Don’t be afraid to confess.
You don’t need just mature friends, you need friends that are filled with the Holy Spirit.
Whatever you hide, God cannot bless.
If you despise wisdom and godly council, you are a foolish person.
