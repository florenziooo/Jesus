Why do you think Jesus cursed the fig tree?

A fig tree (in Israel) is associated by having religion and praise but bear no substance.

We can distinguish a wolf in sheeps clothing. A wolf may look like a sheep but does not function like a sheep.

A Christian could appear performative but bear no fruit—no authenticity of your faith. Because things when go under pressure, you are nowhere to be found.

Small buds - expectation of a fruit.
He was expecting fruit from the tree, when He got closer, the tree bore no fruit.
The leaves represents appearance, and fruits represents substance.

We live in a generation that avoid confrontation. We think confrontation means destruction.

The fig tree looked good from afar but it’s questionable when come close.
Performance gets exposed with proximity.
Discipleship reveals fruit.
We stop performing and we start realigning.

Abide in me and you will bear fruit.
A mango tree does not eat other mangoes. It bears mango fruit so that other people benefit from it.
