## The Threefolds of Ministry

To preach is to proclaim.
Preaching awakens belief.
Teaching strengthens matures faith.
Teaching stabilizes the believer.
Teaching moves faith from excitement to endurance.
Healing demonstrates the Kingdom.
Healing shows the reality of God’s power.
