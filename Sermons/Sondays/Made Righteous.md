There are two extremes of human tendencies.
LEGALISM - I need to work a lot to be made righteous by God.
LICENSE - I have been made righteous by God so I can do whatever I want.
GRACE IS A GIFT. WE ARE UNDESERVED FOR IT.

> [!quote] [[Titus 2#^v11|Titus 2:11-12]] ESV
> “For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation for all people, training us to renounce ungodliness and worldly passions, and to live self-controlled, upright, and godly lives in the present age,”

Grace does not just give you a ticket to heaven.
Grace empowers you to GET OUT OF SIN.
Do not wait for you to die. Live because you have been given grace while walking on this earth, for GRACE teaches you HOW TO LIVE.
