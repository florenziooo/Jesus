## Spiritual Checkup

- Is your heart wounded?
- Is your heart protected?

## Pharisee vs. Woman vs. Jesus

| | Heart |
|---|---|
| **The Pharisee** | judgmental, critical, condemning |
| **The sinful woman** | humble, broken, repentant, full of faith |
| **Jesus** | compassionate, merciful, full of life |

> [!quote] [[Proverbs 4#^v23|Proverbs 4:23]]
> Guard your heart; for everything flows with it.

Before he promotes you, he prepares your heart to be pure, refined, healed and whole to receive that promotion well.

Before outpouring, there was waiting, there was praying.

Is your heart ready for what you are praying for?

## Three P’s

### Position

Where is your heart right now? The woman placed her heart at Jesus’ feet. Oftentimes, we admit that Jesus is Lord, but we do not submit in His lordship.

If you want God to transform you, you need to learn to surrender.

### Posture

What is the attitude of your heart? The posture of the Pharisee was judgmental, critical, condemning. The posture of the sinful woman was humble, broken, repentant, full of faith.

God is not impressed by the outward appearance. God is impressed by the posture of the heart that surrenders.

### Principle

What do you feed your heart with? It may often be public exposure, circle of friends, social media algorithms, etc.

Look at the Parable of the Sower ([[Matthew 13]]).

Just like the soil, what you receive in your heart will determine its harvest.
