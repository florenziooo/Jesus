WORLD: Follow your heart
WORD: Don’t follow your heart

> [!quote] [[Ezekiel 36#^v26|Ezekiel 36:26]] NLT
> “And I will give you a new heart, and I will put a new spirit in you. I will take out your stony, stubborn heart and give you a tender, responsive heart.”

WORLD: Put yourself first; become self-made.
WORD: Commit yourself to the Lord. Honor God. Honor rest.

The world is so into over-consumerism. A lot of us are obsessed to things they didn’t have.

> [!quote] [[Psalm 23#^v1|Psalms 23:1]] NLT
> “The Lord is my shepherd; I have all that I need.”

Peace is the fellowship of God made available for you to enter.

Trust His own timelines, own desires for you.
