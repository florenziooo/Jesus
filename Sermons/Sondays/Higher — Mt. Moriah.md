Moriah - Seen by Yahweh

Testing reveals what is inside of your heart. But God knew it already. Testing is for you to be made aware.

By faith, Abraham believed God. God called him righteous. God gave Abraham Isaac. But God promised Abraham that he will be a father of many nations. Abraham willingly gave Isaac for this covenant. And later then revealed that the offspring of Abraham be done through Isaac.

## You Cannot Die Until the Promise Happens

Isaac could have resist his father’s choices. Isaac willingly submitted. This is parallel to the character of Jesus.

When Isaac asked his father “Where is the Lamb?” EVERYTHING POINTS TO JESUS.

Jehovah Jireh - The Lord sees.
The Lord sees the need before the need arises.
The Lord sees already in order to provide.

What if the Lord already saw your need before you realized you need, all you gotta do is to go up to the mountain in obedience and surrender, so that you will be made aware of God’s character and His provision.

Why do tests exist? So that God will know your character? No, God already knew it. But God tested Abraham to REVEAL Abraham and be made aware of his character.
