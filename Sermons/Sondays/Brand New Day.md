> [!info] Reference — [[Exodus 12#^v3|Exodus 12:3]]

Not every bondage begins with a bad thing. In fact, when bondage begins with a good thing but it takes the throne of God the we have a problem.

One compromise leads to a pattern.
Pattern leads to a stronghold.
Stronghold leads to dependence.
Loss of dependence leads to loss of control.
Loss of control leads to bondage.

Spiritually, bondage doesn’t only concern you, it’s concerned to the generation after you. The enemy attacks vision. The danger is in the comfort and settling in the bondage.
