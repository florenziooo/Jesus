## Kinds of Offerings

### Burnt offering

- symbolizes complete surrender to God.
- Jesus gave his all.

### Grain offering

- represents daily life and work.
- Jesus is the bread of life.

### Peace offering

- symbolizes community, fellowship, and thanksgiving
- one innocent herd animal and bread.
- Jesus is our peace.

### Sin offering

- symbolizing confession
- for unintentional sins
- gives up an animal depending on who the offender is.
- Jesus bore our sins and became sin for us.

### Guilt offering

- mandatory offering to atone for specific sins or damages
- Jesus paid for our sins full.
