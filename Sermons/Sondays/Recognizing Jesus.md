### Deep Dive — Part 2

**Text:** Luke 24:13–35  
**Main Theme:** Recognizing Jesus in Scripture, in our moments of encounter, and in what God is doing.

---

## 1. OPENING TEXT — LUKE 24:13–35

### The Road to Emmaus

Two disciples were traveling from Jerusalem to Emmaus.

- They were talking about everything that had happened.
    
- Jesus Himself came near and walked with them.
    
- **But their eyes were kept from recognizing Him.**
    
- They were sad, disappointed, and confused.
    
- Their expectation had not been fulfilled.
    

> **“We had hoped that he was the one to redeem Israel.”** — Luke 24:21

### Key thought

**You can be walking with Jesus and still fail to recognize Jesus.**

The issue was not that Jesus was absent.

**Jesus was already there.**

The issue was that they could not perceive Him.

---

# 2. THE GOAL OF STUDYING THE WORD IS TO ENCOUNTER CHRIST

Last week: **Fall in love with the Word.**

This week:

> **How do we actually read the Bible?**

Because you can love the Bible and still read it incorrectly.

You can:

- Read too quickly.
    
- Read out of context.
    
- Look only for promises.
    
- Look only for something that makes you feel good.
    
- Look for confirmation of what you already believe.
    
- Read only to find a “word for yourself.”
    
- Approach Scripture with your own expectations already established.
    

### Important warning

> **It is possible to know the Scriptures and still miss Jesus.**

The goal of studying the Bible is not merely:

- knowing more Bible verses,
    
- gaining more information,
    
- becoming more knowledgeable,
    
- knowing Hebrew or Greek,
    
- knowing every Bible story.
    

### The goal is:

> **To encounter the Christ that the Bible reveals.**

To:

- know Him,
    
- walk with Him,
    
- know His ways,
    
- know His heart,
    
- know His character,
    
- know His purposes.
    

---

# 3. YOU CAN BE CLOSE TO JESUS AND STILL NOT UNDERSTAND WHAT GOD IS DOING

The disciples:

- knew Scripture,
    
- had heard Jesus teach,
    
- had experienced His ministry,
    
- were physically walking beside Him,
    

yet **they did not recognize Him.**

### Application

How many moments have we missed because we were too focused on:

- our emotions,
    
- disappointment,
    
- offense,
    
- anger,
    
- our situation,
    
- our expectations?
    

Jesus may be walking beside us while we are too distracted to recognize Him.

### Ask the church:

> **If Jesus walked through the door right now, would you recognize Him?**

How can you recognize someone you don't spend time with?

How can you recognize someone's voice if you don't hear it?

### Key statement

> **You can love God and still be confused.**
> 
> **You can know the Scriptures and still have blind eyes.**

---

# 4. BIBLICAL LITERACY IS NOT ENOUGH — WE NEED UNDERSTANDING

The disciples knew the Scriptures.

They knew:

- Abraham,
    
- Moses,
    
- the Law,
    
- the prophets.
    

Yet they missed Jesus.

### Why?

They knew the individual stories but did not understand **the story.**

The Bible is not merely a collection of disconnected stories.

> **The Bible is one unified story of God's redemptive work that finds fulfillment in Jesus.**

### Knowledge vs. Understanding

Knowledge says:

> “I know what the Bible says.”

Understanding asks:

> “How does this reveal Christ and God's redemptive story?”

---

# 5. THE PHARISEES: THEY HAD THE SCRIPTURES BUT MISSED THE SAVIOR

### John 5:39–40

Jesus said:

> “You search the Scriptures…”

They were not lazy.

They studied.

They investigated.

They knew the text.

**But they missed the One the Scriptures testified about.**

### Key statement

> **They had the Scriptures, but they missed the Savior.**

Bible knowledge without knowing Jesus can produce people who are experts in Scripture but strangers to the spirit and heart of the Word.

You can:

- know about God,
    
- without surrendering to God.
    

You can:

- know information,
    
- without obedience.
    

### James 1:22

> **“Be doers of the word, and not hearers only.”**

Therefore, the question is not:

> **“How much Bible do you know?”**

The question is:

> **“What is the Bible doing to you?”**

Is it:

- changing you?
    
- renewing you?
    
- restoring you?
    
- changing how you think?
    
- changing how you speak?
    
- changing your framework?
    
- giving you a biblical worldview?
    

### Key statement

> **The power is not merely in knowing. The power is in obedience.**
> 
> **The power is not merely in information. It is in surrender.**

---

# 6. OUR EXPECTATIONS CAN KEEP US FROM RECOGNIZING JESUS

The disciples said:

> **“We had hoped…”**

Their problem was not that they had no expectation.

Their expectation was **wrongly framed.**

They expected the Messiah to redeem Israel according to their understanding.

Their expectation became a filter.

### Principle

> **Sometimes our own bias and expectation stop us from encountering what God wants to do.**

When approaching Scripture, we can already have something in our hearts that we are unwilling to lay down.

Therefore:

> **Do not make Scripture submit to your emotions. Submit your emotions to Scripture.**

Be careful not to call something “conviction” when it may simply be your emotion.

---

# 7. JESUS' RESPONSE TO THEIR CONFUSION: HE OPENED THE SCRIPTURES

Jesus could have simply said:

> “Guys, it's Me. I'm alive.”

But He didn't.

### Luke 24:27

Beginning with Moses and all the prophets, Jesus interpreted the Scriptures concerning Himself.

### Important principle

When the disciples were confused about Jesus:

> **Jesus opened the Scriptures.**

He did not merely give them an experience.

He taught them the Word.

### Therefore:

When you are:

- discouraged,
    
- disappointed,
    
- confused,
    
- frustrated,
    

**what do you open?**

Social media?

Group chats?

Opinions?

Or Scripture?

### Challenge

When you open Scripture, don't ask:

> “How can this support what I already believe?”

Ask:

> **“God, what have You actually said?”**

# 8. READ THE BIBLE THROUGH THE LENS OF JESUS

### Luke 24:27

Jesus interpreted the Scriptures concerning Himself.

Therefore:

> **Learn to see the whole story through the lens of Christ.**

The Bible is one great story:

### Creation

God creates and everything is good.

### Fall

Sin enters.

Humanity rebels.

Death enters.

### Promise

Genesis 3:15 introduces the promise of the coming seed.

### Covenant

God establishes covenant with Abraham.

### Israel

God forms a people and works through Israel.

### Law / Priesthood / Sacrifice / Kingship

These point forward toward Christ.

### Messiah

Israel anticipates the coming Messiah.

### Jesus

The Word becomes flesh.

### Cross

Jesus is crucified.

Sin is dealt with.

### Resurrection

Jesus rises.

### Ascension

Jesus ascends.

### Holy Spirit / Church

Jesus pours out His Spirit upon His people.

### Future Hope

We anticipate the coming of Christ, His Kingdom, and the new creation.

### Key statement

> **Understand the story. See Christ in it.**

---

# 9. HOW DOES THE OLD TESTAMENT POINT TO JESUS?

There are several ways:

### 1. It points to Christ

**Genesis 3:15**

The promised seed points forward to Christ.

### 2. It foreshadows Christ

Examples:

- Passover lamb → Christ
    
- Sacrifices → Christ
    
- Offerings → Christ
    

### 3. It prepares us for Christ

The Law prepares people for the coming Savior.

### 4. It finds fulfillment in Christ

The story ultimately finds fulfillment in Jesus.

### Examples from the sermon

- **Genesis** — Jesus as the promised seed
    
- **Exodus** — Jesus as the Passover Lamb and Deliverer
    
- **Deuteronomy** — Jesus as the promised land / fulfillment
    
- **Isaiah** — Jesus as the suffering servant
    
- **Zechariah** — Jesus as the cornerstone
    

### Key question when studying Scripture:

> **How does this passage point toward, prepare for, foreshadow, or find fulfillment in Jesus?**

# 10. HOW TO READ THE BIBLE JESUS' WAY

## STEP 1 — READ

Read what the passage actually says.

Don't immediately interpret.

Don't immediately try to make everything about yourself.

Read it again.

Read slowly.

---

## STEP 2 — OBSERVE

Ask:

- Who is speaking?
    
- Who is being spoken to?
    
- When was this spoken?
    
- Where did this happen?
    
- What was the cultural context?
    
- What do I actually see?
    

### Don't immediately ask:

> “What does this mean to me?”

First ask:

> **“What does this actually mean?”**

---

## STEP 3 — LOCATE IT IN THE STORY

Ask:

- Where are we in God's redemptive story?
    
- Creation?
    
- Fall?
    
- Covenant?
    
- Law?
    
- Priesthood?
    
- Sacrifice?
    
- Kingship?
    
- Promise?
    
- Coming Messiah?
    
- Christ's ministry?
    
- Cross?
    
- Resurrection?
    
- Church?
    
- Future Kingdom?
    

Then ask:

> **Where is Christ in this story?**

---

## STEP 4 — SEE CHRIST

Don't force Jesus into every verse imaginatively.

Instead, understand the whole story so that you can properly recognize how the passage relates to Christ.

### Example: David and Goliath

Don't immediately make yourself David.

First ask:

> **How does this passage fit into God's story?**

Then consider its Christ-centered significance and application.

### Key principle

> **Christ-centered reading does not mean forcing Christ into every verse. It means recognizing that Scripture already presents God's redemptive story fulfilled in Christ.**

# 12. LOGOS AND RHEMA — DON'T CHASE RHEMA, CHASE JESUS

The sermon uses the distinction between:

### LOGOS

The revealed Word / expression of God.

Jesus is the ultimate Logos.

### RHEMA

A specific word highlighted or illuminated by the Holy Spirit for a specific season, purpose, or moment.

### Illustration: The Well and the Cup

Imagine:

- **The well** = the revealed Word of God.
    
- **The cup** = a specific portion taken from the well.
    

The cup is not a different substance.

It comes from the well.

Likewise:

> **Rhema should come out of the Logos.**

It is not:

- another gospel,
    
- another revelation disconnected from Scripture,
    
- something that contradicts God's Word.
    

### Important warning

> **Rhema never contradicts the Logos.**

Therefore:

> **Stop chasing rhema. Chase Jesus.**

Don't constantly seek:

- “Give me a prophetic word.”
    
- “Give me a new revelation.”
    
- “Tell me what God is saying about me.”
    

Instead:

- pursue Jesus,
    
- love the Word,
    
- walk with the Holy Spirit.
    

If you need a specific word, the Holy Spirit can illuminate what is already in the Word.

### Key statement

> **The problem is: what can the Holy Spirit illuminate if there is nothing in you?**

### Luke 24:28–29

Jesus acted as though He would continue walking.

The disciples said:

> **“Stay with us.”**

### Principle

Jesus did not force Himself upon them.

There was room for them to invite Him.

And when they invited Him:

> **He went in to stay with them.**

### Application

Do you make room for Jesus?

Do you create space where He can reveal Himself?

# 15. STILLNESS IS NECESSARY FOR ENCOUNTER

Jesus revealed Himself to them while they were **at the table**.

Not while they were rushing somewhere.

### Key instruction:

> **Sit down. Be still.**

Stillness does not mean emptying your mind.

Instead, meditation involves:

- focusing on the Word,
    
- thinking about the Word,
    
- confessing the Word,
    
- speaking the Word over your life.
    

### Application

How can intimacy with Jesus grow if we only give Him rushed minutes?

We are always:

- late to school,
    
- late to work,
    
- rushing through the day,
    
- distracted.
    

### Question

> **Do you actually have room in your life for God to reveal Himself to you?**

# 23. CLOSING — “SPEAK, LORD”

### 1 Samuel 3

Samuel was young and serving under Eli.

He heard a voice:

> **“Samuel.”**

He thought Eli was calling him.

Eventually Eli recognized that God was calling Samuel.

Eli instructed him:

> **“Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.”**

### Our posture should be the same.

Not:

> “Speak, Lord, because I need a new prophecy.”

Not:

> “Speak, Lord, because I need a new revelation.”

Not:

> “Speak, Lord, because I need something to preach.”

But:

> **“Speak, Lord, for Your servant is listening.”**

### Servant means:

- I will obey.
    
- I will submit.
    
- I will listen.
    
- I will respond.
    
- I will not argue with what God instructs me to do.
