> [!quote] [[Psalm 30#^v5|Psalms 30:5]] AMP
> “For His anger is but for a moment, His favor is for a lifetime. Weeping may endure for a night, But a shout of joy comes in the morning. [2 Cor 4:17]”

Favor is designed by God; tied to your assignment. If you align yourself towards God, favor will come its way naturally.
Favor is like faith.

One encounter of favor is worth years of labor.
Favor is not fair.

“The Lord was with Joseph, and he [even though a slave] became a successful and prosperous man; and he was in the house of his master, the Egyptian.” - [[Genesis 39#^v2|Genesis 39:2]] AMP
If we are with the Lord, we will walk in favor.

Favor will give assistance in whatever you’re doing.
We need to stop seeing problems as God’s curses. We need to see problems as platforms for promotion.

The Father is pleased when we step in the throne of grace and ASK.
God knows your desires. However, alot of us don’t ask the Father because we are too prideful.
Stop chasing favor. Let favor chase you when you are after God’s heart.
