God’s favor comes with an invitation. God’s favor comes in unexpected and inconvenient places where you were never prepared.

It’s easy to say “I trust You, Lord!” but it’s hard to keep showing up for Him—even in your lowest moments.

When God invites you, he will guide you with STEP BY STEP instructions without revealing His whole plan, but what pleases the Lord is faith. But decision is ON YOU.

> [!quote] [[Luke 1#^v29|Luke 1:29-30]] NLT
> “Confused and disturbed, Mary tried to think what the angel could mean. “Don’t be afraid, Mary,” the angel told her, “for you have found favor with God!”

The instruction was “DO NOT BE AFRAID!”

Success is when you OBEY GOD! Favor is about the Lord’s presence.

Allow the Lord to shape you. Let God’s Word dictate you.
