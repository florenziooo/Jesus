# Romans 8

> [!abstract] New Living Translation
> Only the verses cited elsewhere in this vault are reproduced.
> Source: [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8&version=NLT) · retrieved 2026-09-02

**5** Those who are dominated by the sinful nature think about sinful things, but those who are controlled by the Holy Spirit think about things that please the Spirit. ^v5
**6** So letting your sinful nature control your mind leads to death. But letting the Spirit control your mind leads to life and peace. ^v6
**7** For the sinful nature is always hostile to God. It never did obey God’s laws, and it never will. ^v7
**8** That’s why those who are still under the control of their sinful nature can never please God. ^v8
**9** But you are not controlled by your sinful nature. You are controlled by the Spirit if you have the Spirit of God living in you. (And remember that those who do not have the Spirit of Christ living in them do not belong to him at all.) ^v9
**10** And Christ lives within you, so even though your body will die because of sin, the Spirit gives you life because you have been made right with God. ^v10
**11** The Spirit of God, who raised Jesus from the dead, lives in you. And just as God raised Christ Jesus from the dead, he will give life to your mortal bodies by this same Spirit living within you. ^v11
**38** And I am convinced that nothing can ever separate us from God’s love. Neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither our fears for today nor our worries about tomorrow—not even the powers of hell can separate us from God’s love. ^v38
**39** No power in the sky above or in the earth below—indeed, nothing in all creation will ever be able to separate us from the love of God that is revealed in Christ Jesus our Lord. ^v39
