**11** For the grace of God has been revealed, bringing salvation to all people. ^v11
**12** And we are instructed to turn from godless living and sinful pleasures. We should live in this evil world with wisdom, righteousness, and devotion to God, ^v12
**13** while we look forward with hope to that wonderful day when the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ, will be revealed. ^v13
