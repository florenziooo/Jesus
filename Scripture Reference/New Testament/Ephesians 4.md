# Ephesians 4

> [!abstract] New Living Translation
> Only the verses cited elsewhere in this vault are reproduced.
> Source: [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4&version=NLT) · retrieved 2026-09-02

**11** Now these are the gifts Christ gave to the church: the apostles, the prophets, the evangelists, and the pastors and teachers. ^v11
**12** Their responsibility is to equip God’s people to do his work and build up the church, the body of Christ. ^v12
**22** throw off your old sinful nature and your former way of life, which is corrupted by lust and deception. ^v22
**23** Instead, let the Spirit renew your thoughts and attitudes. ^v23
**24** Put on your new nature, created to be like God—truly righteous and holy. ^v24
**25** So stop telling lies. Let us tell our neighbors the truth, for we are all parts of the same body. ^v25
**26** And “don’t sin by letting anger control you.” Don’t let the sun go down while you are still angry, ^v26
**27** for anger gives a foothold to the devil. ^v27
**28** If you are a thief, quit stealing. Instead, use your hands for good hard work, and then give generously to others in need. ^v28
**29** Don’t use foul or abusive language. Let everything you say be good and helpful, so that your words will be an encouragement to those who hear them. ^v29
**30** And do not bring sorrow to God’s Holy Spirit by the way you live. Remember, he has identified you as his own, guaranteeing that you will be saved on the day of redemption. ^v30
**31** Get rid of all bitterness, rage, anger, harsh words, and slander, as well as all types of evil behavior. ^v31
**32** Instead, be kind to each other, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God through Christ has forgiven you. ^v32
