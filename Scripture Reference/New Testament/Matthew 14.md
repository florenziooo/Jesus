**4** John had been telling Herod, “It is against God’s law for you to marry her.” ^v4
**5** Herod wanted to kill John, but he was afraid of a riot, because all the people believed John was a prophet. ^v5
**14** Jesus saw the huge crowd as he stepped from the boat, and he had compassion on them and healed their sick. ^v14
**15** That evening the disciples came to him and said, “This is a remote place, and it’s already getting late. Send the crowds away so they can go to the villages and buy food for themselves.” ^v15
**16** But Jesus said, “That isn’t necessary—you feed them.” ^v16
**24** Meanwhile, the disciples were in trouble far away from land, for a strong wind had risen, and they were fighting heavy waves. ^v24
**25** About three o’clock in the morning Jesus came toward them, walking on the water. ^v25
**26** When the disciples saw him walking on the water, they were terrified. In their fear, they cried out, “It’s a ghost!” ^v26
**27** But Jesus spoke to them at once. “Don’t be afraid,” he said. “Take courage. I am here!” ^v27
**28** Then Peter called to him, “Lord, if it’s really you, tell me to come to you, walking on the water.” ^v28
**29** “Yes, come,” Jesus said. So Peter went over the side of the boat and walked on the water toward Jesus. ^v29
**30** But when he saw the strong wind and the waves, he was terrified and began to sink. “Save me, Lord!” he shouted. ^v30
**31** Jesus immediately reached out and grabbed him. “You have so little faith,” Jesus said. “Why did you doubt me?” ^v31
