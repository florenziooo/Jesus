**1** 18 Later on, in the third year of the drought, the Lord said to Elijah, “Go and present yourself to King Ahab. Tell him that I will soon send rain!” ^v1
**2** So Elijah went to appear before Ahab. Meanwhile, the famine had become very severe in Samaria. ^v2
**3** So Ahab summoned Obadiah, who was in charge of the palace. (Obadiah was a devoted follower of the Lord. ^v3
**16** So Obadiah went to tell Ahab that Elijah had come, and Ahab went out to meet Elijah. ^v16
**17** When Ahab saw him, he exclaimed, “So, is it really you, you troublemaker of Israel?” ^v17
**18** “I have made no trouble for Israel,” Elijah replied. “You and your family are the troublemakers, for you have refused to obey the commands of the Lord and have worshiped the images of Baal instead. ^v18
**21** Then Elijah stood in front of them and said, “How much longer will you waver, hobbling between two opinions? If the Lord is God, follow him! But if Baal is God, then follow him!” But the people were completely silent. ^v21
**36** At the usual time for offering the evening sacrifice, Elijah the prophet walked up to the altar and prayed, “O Lord, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, prove today that you are God in Israel and that I am your servant. Prove that I have done all this at your command. ^v36
**37** O Lord, answer me! Answer me so these people will know that you, O Lord, are God and that you have brought them back to yourself.” ^v37
**39** And when all the people saw it, they fell face down on the ground and cried out, “The Lord —he is God! Yes, the Lord is God!” ^v39
