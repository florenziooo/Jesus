# Ecclesiastes 5

> [!abstract] New Living Translation
> Only the verses cited elsewhere in this vault are reproduced.
> Source: [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ecclesiastes+5&version=NLT) · retrieved 2026-09-02

**11** The more you have, the more people come to help you spend it. So what good is wealth—except perhaps to watch it slip through your fingers! ^v11
**12** People who work hard sleep well, whether they eat little or much. But the rich seldom get a good night’s sleep. ^v12
**19** And it is a good thing to receive wealth from God and the good health to enjoy it. To enjoy your work and accept your lot in life—this is indeed a gift from God. ^v19
**20** God keeps such people so busy enjoying life that they take no time to brood over the past. ^v20
