# Psalm 78

> [!abstract] New Living Translation
> Only the verses cited elsewhere in this vault are reproduced.
> Source: [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+78&version=NLT) · retrieved 2026-09-02

**5** For he issued his laws to Jacob; he gave his instructions to Israel. He commanded our ancestors to teach them to their children, ^v5
**6** so the next generation might know them— even the children not yet born— and they in turn will teach their own children. ^v6
**7** So each generation should set its hope anew on God, not forgetting his glorious miracles and obeying his commands. ^v7
