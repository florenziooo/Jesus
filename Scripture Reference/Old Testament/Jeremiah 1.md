# Jeremiah 1

> [!abstract] New Living Translation
> Only the verses cited elsewhere in this vault are reproduced.
> Source: [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah+1&version=NLT) · retrieved 2026-09-02

**5** “I knew you before I formed you in your mother’s womb. Before you were born I set you apart and appointed you as my prophet to the nations.” ^v5
**6** “O Sovereign Lord,” I said, “I can’t speak for you! I’m too young!” ^v6
**7** The Lord replied, “Don’t say, ‘I’m too young,’ for you must go wherever I send you and say whatever I tell you. ^v7
**8** And don’t be afraid of the people, for I will be with you and will protect you. I, the Lord, have spoken!” ^v8
**9** Then the Lord reached out and touched my mouth and said, “Look, I have put my words in your mouth! ^v9
**10** Today I appoint you to stand up against nations and kingdoms. Some you must uproot and tear down, destroy and overthrow. Others you must build up and plant.” ^v10
