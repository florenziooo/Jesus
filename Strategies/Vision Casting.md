Looking to the Future.

Jesus was constantly putting the big picture in front of His disciples.

A compelling vision is the driving purpose for our actions
- If people don't know why they're doing something, it's very difficult for them to get motivated about it.

Vision inspires people to participate in *something bigger than themselves*.

People need to know what the *end-state* is in order to achieve it.

---
### How do I cast vision?
- The Ways and Words of Jesus
	- Not just hearing the Word; but actively obeying the Word.
- Need to cast the vision often.
	- Constantly remind your disciples about their vision.
	- Vision is infused over a consistent and long period of time.
	- Cast vision in bits many times, not a grand one in one time.
- Use the whole of Scripture (passages)
	- Matthew 4:19
	- Matthew 28:18-20
	- Acts 1:8
	- 2 Timothy 2:2
	- Moses to Joshua
	- Elijah to Elisha
	- Jesus to the 3, 12, 72, 120, 500
- Do it simply.
	- Give the small pieces/parts that they can digest.
- Do it profoundly.
	- Not just simple, but put significant an eternal difference to the world.
- Go for the heart.
	- If it's just about facts and data, it's not helpful.
	- Relationship first, get a hold of his heart.
	- If you capture the heart, the feet and the hands will follow
- Look at your own context.
	- How do I apply the Scripture and obey in modern times?
