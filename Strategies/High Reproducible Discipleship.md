> *We can paint beautiful landscapes!*

But your disciples cannot paint yet.
So you need to paint by the numbers.

Before the art, before the spontaneity...
Comes system, preparation, and process.

They need something simple; something reproducible.
##### The main thing is to keep the main thing the main thing. And the main thing is not a thing, it's a Who.
# Jesus.
---
*Remember that THIS IS A METHOD. WE CANNOT LOSE **JESUS** IN THE PROCESS.*

1. PAST
	1. Prayer
		1. Start by *prayer*.
	2. How are you doing?
		1. Talk about that and *be with them* first before diving in.
	3. Accountability goals from last week.
			1. Jesus Up-in-and-out
				1. **CENTER** - Jesus
				2. **UP** - Relationship with God.
					1. *I want to read a chapter in the bible for the day.*
					2. *I want to pray everyday.*
				3. **IN** - Relationship with our brothers and sisters, family.
					1. *I want to be a part of challenging and encouraging fellowship some times a week.*
				4. **OUT** - Relationship with people who are not in the kingdom yet.
					1. *I want to master my testimony sharing in 2 minutes.*
2. PRESENT
	1. Teach them a new lesson.
		1. The Prayer Hand.
		2. Repent and Believe.
		3. Discipleship.
		4. Leadership.
		5. Look at Lifeclass
3. FUTURE
	1. Practice Key Point
		1. Take a key point from the lesson and help them practice it right on the spot.
		2. Watch your time.
		3. It puts immediate application into the process.
	2. Vision Casting
		1. What's the big picture?
		2. How do we become like Jesus?
	3. Accountability goals for next week
		1. That comes out of the lesson.
		2. Readjust goals for next week (JESUS UP-IN-AND-OUT)
		3. Ask next week about it.
4. PRAYER
	1. Close with prayer.
