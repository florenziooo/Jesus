## Effective Leadership
---
The best leaders are the ones who serve the most.
Don't just serve pastors, leaders, and ministry heads.
Serve your people, flock, family.

Attendance is never a measurement of effective leadership.
Leadership is measured by when you see people growing; progressing...
With or without you.

Ask yourself:
- Is it a me problem?
- Is it a you problem?
- Is it a we problem?
- Is it a God problem?

Pray, fast for it. Invite God to the table.

## Struggling with Patterns and Identity
---
>*How can you address someone who is struggling with identity; and keep going into patterns—that eventually convinced himself to be distant; shameful—because he feels like he made no progress on his journey?*

A lie repeatedly told could become a belief system.

### Separate the sin and the sinner.

Connect with him as sinner to sinner.
We are both sinners.
But I am renewed, compelled, yielded, and now whole.
Because I confronted the sin. Now the sinner became a saint.


### Confront the sin

Identify the root cause of the sin.
Make it very separate from the sinner.

>*"I don't see you as one."*


### Confront the lie.

> *The thief comes to steal, kill, and destroy.* - John 10:10

Do you wonder why the devil keeps attacking and lying to you? Why?
Because you have a calling.
When people see you, they see Jesus in you.
You are a threat to the camp of the enemy.
Every word that you speak the truth startles the enemy.
So when you see attacks or lies, don't be shocked.


### Who are you submitted to?

- Animalism
	- A living thing submitted to its instincts and desires.
- A person of faith
	- A person recognizing that you are a spiritual being.
	- That no one can actually satisfy you but the love of God.

### How does grace come in?

>*"Do you know why I am saying this to you?
>Because I love you. As a spiritual father.*
>*That I know that you could change.*
>*If nobody said that you could do it, I believe you can do it."*

Paint them a vision.

### Love the sinner.

Ubani.

>*"This kind of healing is not only limited to this table.
>It takes a journey.
>Maybe today I am giving you a spiritual buffet.
>Maybe some food are hard to swallow.
>But nothing is more powerful that the daily bread.
>You need to have constant infilling of Jesus and His word.
>And just so you know...
>That I am willing to walk with you."*


## Why they are so fired up?
---
>*Because they feel needed. They feel called.*

#### What's the version of their vision?
- Could it welcome somebody else?
- Could it make somebody else feel needed?
- Could it bless somebody else?

Make sure that every time we gather, that they feel needed.
This is the very birthplace of ***calling***.

The purpose of Lifeclass is to produce a *disciple*.
What is one of the disciples' product? Involvement—ministry.
