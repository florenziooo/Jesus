> [!quote] [[Matthew 14#^v14|Matthew 14:14]]
> **14** When Jesus landed and saw a large crowd, he had compassion on them and healed their sick.

Ministry is not about performance or how good you are at doing ministry. It’s starts with how you see people the way Jesus did. Jesus did not have to show up being powerful, but he acted out of compassion. So, we must do the same.

> [!quote] [[Matthew 14#^v15|Matthew 14:15]]
> **15** As evening approached, the disciples came to him and said, “This is a remote place, and it’s already getting late. Send the crowds away, so they can go to the villages and buy themselves some food.”

> [!quote] [[Matthew 14#^v16|Matthew 14:16]]
> **16** Jesus replied, “They do not need to go away. You give them something to eat.”

Both Jesus and the disciples were aware of the great multitude and aware of their needs.

When the disciples said, “Send the crowds away”, that’s not inherently selfish. They probably were geniunely worried about the people’s needs. It’s just that their care was limited by what they believed is possible.

Even though the disciples have seen numerous miracles, in that circumstance they opt to think that “hey, we can’t solve this, maybe sending them to villages can”. But despite after learning that Jesus’ cousin was killed, Jesus was still moved by compassion that he told the people to not go away and feed them there instead. But when Jesus broke the bread and multiplied the food, He then hands them into the disciples and distributes them to the people.

The disciples were just being practical, and I can’t blame them.

Oftentimes, although we often believe that God is on our side, we can’t help but to think of alternative ways to solve a problem.

We may think that “the problem might too big for us”. But we often forget to submit this problem to God. We may be even blind to watch Him at work.

To us we see this problem as a problem of scarcity, overwhelm, and limitations. But Jesus see this problem as an opportunity to have faith in Him to provide.

For those who are needy, don’t send them away. Bring them to Jesus. For the leaders, do not be afraid to seek first the Kingdom.
