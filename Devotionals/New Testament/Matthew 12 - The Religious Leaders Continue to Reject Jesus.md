## A Discussion About the Sabbath

When the Pharisees called out Jesus for letting his disciples doing what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath. Jesus replies assertively,

“Yet I say to you that in this place there is One greater than the temple.”
“For the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.”

This shows a powerful revelation:

Relationship matters more than religion.
Mercy supersedes law.
Heart before performance.

For Jesus is not just the temple. He is MORE than that.
Therefore, if we honor the temple (religion) and its laws, we should honor Jesus even more.
If the temple is a place for worship, we should worship Jesus even more.

If we get to know more about Jesus, then that is all that really matters. We don’t blindly follow the law. He is the One the law points to.

## Jesus Heals on the Sabbath

When the Pharisees accuses Jesus for trying to heal on the Sabbath, Jesus replied:

“What man is there among you who has one sheep, and if it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will not lay hold of it and lift it out? Of how much more value then is a man than a sheep?”

Real love does not wait for permission. If there is someone in need, have compassion to help them. Lift them out the pit they fell into. Because of Jesus Christ within us, we become hope to those in need.

## Jesus, God’s Chosen Servant

“But when Jesus knew it, He withdrew from there”

When opposition grows, Jesus withdraws—not out of cowardice, He did not fight back because it is just simply not the right time to culminate His ministry. But He kept on healing people out of mercy and compassion.

Most of us get discouraged of doing good because the world opposes because of their beliefs. But we often forget that we are God’s servants as Jesus is.

We often forget that God
is the god of order
is the god of seasons
is the god of suddenlies.

Just like Jesus, we don’t want to come out of purpose as servants prematurely. We need to allow God to refine us to get closer to our purpose. And sometimes that will appear through tests, persecutions, and uncertainties. These things require faith.

“He will not quarrel nor cry out, nor will anyone hear His voice in the streets”.

There is a gentle and humbling way to carry out our service. We don’t need to talk loudly or study a lot of Scripture to prove something. When we get to know Jesus for who he really is and act according to his teachings. People will see naturally that the Spirit of God is upon us. The best Bible they can read is your life and how you live it. And the posture of your heart reaches far more than what pretense could ever do.

## Jesus and the Prince of Demons

> [!quote] [[Matthew 12#^v24|Matthew 12:24]]
> Now when the Pharisees heard it they said, “This fellow does not cast out demons except by Beelzebub, the ruler of the demons.”

It is heartbreaking to see that there will be people who will misinterpret you and labeling you as rebellious because God frees you in such a way that it might oppose them.

Anyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, either in this age or in the age to come.” — [[Matthew 12#^v32|Matthew 12:32]]

This does not mean saying a bad word about the Spirit or struggling to believe. It is about deciding to reject the Holy Spirit’s work and labeling it as evil. But such a decision like that is not a rookie mistake—it comes from the heart that’s willing to settle to be slowly hardened.

For forgiveness requires repentance. Repentance requires conviction; conviction comes from the Holy Spirit. So if you say “The Spirit working on me is evil” not once or twice but fully ingrained in your heart, then there’s no way you can ever feel repentant.

## The Sign of Jonah

> [!quote] [[Matthew 12#^v38|Matthew 12:38]]
> “Teacher, we want to see a sign from You.”

Faith that always demands more proof is often a faith that refuses to surrender.

One resurrection is more than enough of a sign.

For Nineveh that Jonah hated saw no miracles, no signs, no Scripture—just a reluctant sermon by Jonah they repented.

But Jonah had to be in a brink of destruction before He obeyed.
But Jesus gave His life willingly.

However, the Pharisees already saw miracles and signs, and yet still refused to repent.

Ask yourself honestly:
If one resurrection is already a miracle, what stops you from surrendering?
Are you waiting for YOUR kind of miracle in order to believe?
Are you still going to delay obedience until something strips you deeply?

One resurrection is enough to pick a side. Staying in the gray area is impossible.

## The True Family of Jesus

“For whoever does the will of My Father in heaven is My brother and sister and mother.”

Jesus explains how the Kingdom of Heaven works. Spiritual family is not built by bloodline, it is built by obedience.

It is who will walk with you in the journey of pursuing God. His explanation also invites even those who are against Him. They could still come and be a part of His family. As long as we are aligned, we become brothers and sisters in the eyes of God.
