> [!quote] [[John 21]]
> This chapter focuses about Peter’s discouragement when he denied Jesus, now that He was finally resurrected. Peter goes back to the thing he does best—“I’m going fishing.”

Sins to Forsake
Relying to our own strength without trusting Jesus even though he is not physically present. The disciples carry on fishing without Jesus and caught nothing throughout the night.
Doubt and discouragement. Peter went onto fishing in despair after he failed the Lord. He forgot that he can believe in Jesus again to restore him from his failures.
Divided love when it should be for Christ first.
Comparing yourself with others. When Jesus told Peter how his death would glorify God, Peter asked John’s destiny. We must leave the envy and just focus on our calling.

Jesus never fails to provide even after our failures. Oftentimes we say our vows to the Lord as if we already knew to ourselves that we will never disappoint Him. But we often forget that we are human beings too. Jesus only wants where our hearts lie, and that’s all that really mattered.

Jesus restores us and gives us a mission in life despite failing so many times. Our past biggest mistakes don’t matter anymore because of the cross. He still gives us grace to fulfill our own mission He gave—by using our bodies, our talents, our sacrifices, whether it is simple or strange, He will be with us and equip us whether painfully or rejoicingly to transform us and bear fruit to expand His heavenly kingdom.
