>*'And my message and my preaching were very plain. Rather than using clever and persuasive speeches, I relied only on the power of the Holy Spirit. ' — 1 Corinthians 2:4*

Paul expresses his way of spreading the Gospel message to the people of Corinth—a wealthy city whose moral standards are decaying—where rhetorics and charisma are often praised.

Paul was challenged with fear and trembling when he came to Corinth. He did not prepare any wisdom, allegory, or any kind of rhetoric. He was simply spreading the message purely on Jesus who was crucified.

Paul challenged himself by relying on the Holy Spirit instead of human intellect and persuasive speeches.

### Wisdom not from the earth, but from the Spirit.

Ever notice that your decisions become better in long term now that you have history with Christ?

Because the wisdom that you get right now is from the Spirit.
- The Scripture that you read and cling on.
- Christian brothers and sisters you've been journeying with.
- Their growth and your growth that you're witnessing due to God's revelation of truth.
- The environment that you're surrounded.
- The obedience and surrender that you did in the past.

All of that comes from the Spirit, and not from the rulers of the world who seeks to steal, kill, and destroy.

And sure, now that you are in Christ also means choosing decisions that may look less appealing than the alternative.
It may seem costly, painful, and agonizing at first.

But you kept hanging on.
Not because you can.
It's because you knew the truth that you cannot.
But you knew about God's faithfulness and love.
You knew that these are parts of sanctification and process.
You knew that it's for strengthening perseverance and for character-building.
You knew that you need a divine partnership with God.

Only heavenly people can hear heavenly wisdom.
Unbelievers cannot.
Heavenly wisdom can wait. Preach about Jesus first.

### You cannot win everyone, and that's okay.

The Gospel is a gift from God to anyone.
Evangelism is done by a human filled by the Spirit.
Salvation can happen if someone admits that their spirit is in need of a Savior.

Even Jesus cannot help someone who doesn't want to accept Him.
Let alone someone who doesn't believe in what He can do.

Even so, no one even thought that God's plan would turn out this way—in His way.

>*'For since the world began, no ear has heard and no eye has seen a God like you, who works for those who wait for him! '  — Isaiah 64:4*

How can you explain the spiritual truth to someone who is not spiritual?
They will mock you, and that's okay. They can't see something that only you can see.

Only a person with spiritual thirst and unexplainable faith—regardless of his past, whether he verbally acknowledges it or not—can truly recognize the Spirit of God and His Message.

##### But know this:

You will not know that someone is in need of a Savior if you don't try to spread it.
If God has been evident to transform your life, you have no problem in spreading it to people who are in need of it.

##### You have no idea that even you are in need of a Savior—until someone shows the Jesus that is in him to you.
##### How can you expect someone you're praying for to be transformed if you don't even show to him what Jesus has done for you and what He can do for him?

The enemy can distort the things around you, even the Scripture you read.
You don't need to know everything about the Scripture.
Your Message about the Gospel will bring weight by the things the enemy cannot steal. ***Your testimony about Jesus***.

Han-aya imong testimony.
Practice speaking it repetitively.
Know it by heart.
You carry the message, but only the Spirit can bring weight to it.
