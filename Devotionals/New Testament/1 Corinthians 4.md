> 'Now, a person who is put in charge as a manager must be faithful. '  — 1 Corinthians 4:2

Paul addressed the churches of Corinth that they have been exalting one apostle over the other.

Paul exclaimed that the apostles are not to be regarded as masters, but as servants of Christ.

The word Paul used is *hyperetes* for a servant. It means under-rower. It means that one acts faithfully under the commands of the captain—without asking many questions. This is akin to the chief engineer of the ship engine to the captain's direction. The chief engineer below the engine cannot see what the captain sees from above. So the chief engineer relies on the captains commands without question.

Likewise, as servants of Christ, we must not judge hastily in our own intuition but faithfully be sensitive to God's voice. We must be familiar with the rhythm of God's heart. In the end, God will judge us and reveal to us our secret desire and therefore praise us where praise is due.

The servants of Christ are not beneath nor above.
The servants of Christ are not looking for a sphere of influence on himself.
The reason you're a servant right now is because a servant of Christ preached to you about the Good News, and you're now sustained by Him and not the servant.

You have all the resources at your disposal. There is no point anymore to idolize any human. If you want to grow up, live by God's power.
