> [!quote] [[Galatians 2#^v20|Galatians 2:20]]
> "I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me."

A lot of the times man gud we often confuse the identity and responsibility because “we do it to be loved and accepted by God”, but it must be that “we do it BECAUSE we are loved.” It’s easier man gud especially like high-achievers like myself to be burnout, and when we burnout we tend to feel guilty because we didn’t do enough, or dali ta ma discourage kay it didn’t please the people. But the gospel says Christ’s grace is enough so we get to have the new identity of like… Christ living in me. That’s why para nako that devotionals are really important kay diha gyud mo convict ang Holy Spirit nato personally and dili ta madala sa opinions sa uban tawo, even the leaders as well.

We also have to call out and rebuke people who maybe intentionally or unintentionally distorted grace. Peter’s actions confuse other people about grace. But because Peter is one of the church pillars, ni kumpyansa rapud mga tawo.

Resting in grace is hard for high-achievers. We want to contribute to our salvation. But we must accept that we contribute nothing but the sin that made salvation necessary.

We need to be PERSONALLY DISCERNING gyud.
