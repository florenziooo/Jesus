**Central Scripture:**
> The Savior—yes, the Messiah, the Lord—has been born today in Bethlehem, the city of David!
> — Luke 2:11


### God's sovereignty over earthly rulers.

When the Roman emperor Ceasar Augustus issued a census for Roman citizens to get taxed, a secular decree did not stop God's Word to come about a son.
The census unintentionally brought Joseph and Mary together in Bethlehem (the city of David) where Jesus was conceived.


### God sometimes reveals extraordinary things to ordinary ones.
##### You would never know God's movements (Isaiah 55:8)
You would think God planned to save you from the oppression of Rome. **But He came to save you from the oppression of sin.**
You would think God would send angels to inform the scholars. **But He chose to send the good news to the low-income shepherds instead.**
You would think firstborn sons would be entitled to birthright. **But God might favor the youngest ones instead.**
You would think God would call the high leaders to help bring about the kingdom agenda. **But God chose tax collectors and fisherman and zealots instead.**
You would think a king is to be born in a grand palace. **Jesus the ultimate King was born in a rocky manger.**
You would think that nothing good would come out of Nazareth. **But God proved you wrong.**
You would think God would show His terror for sinners to fear him. **But He chose to identify with sinners with love**.
You would think that God calls the qualified. **But God qualifies the called.**
You would think that the enemy has you now. But Jesus said you are His.

God has the sovereignty to move through the mundane people and mundane things. What seems to be impossible for you is possible with God. Why would you hold back your hopes, when you trust a God who moves in mysterious ways? There are some things that God does that you do not need to know about. The least you could do is just trust Him.


### Jesus, being slowly revealed as more than just a political figure

##### Snugly in strips of cloth
> 'She gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him **snugly in strips of cloth** and laid him in a manger, because there was no lodging available for them. '  
> — Luke 2:7

The **snugly strips of cloth** were a unique sign told by the angels to the shepherds out of all firstborns sons to which they came to see and worship.

Shepherds who had a reputation of being lowly and not worthy to be listened to, instantly tells everyone that the Messiah is here—and they shouted with joy, knowing that they had a glimpse of hope toward humanity, or at least the freedom of the people Israel.

The angels, shepherds, Simeon, and Anna? That slowly reveals that the baby carried by Mary is not simply any other baby. Jesus is a light to reveal God to the nations. And He is the glory of the Israelites.

##### Simeon prophesied
> Then Simeon blessed them, and he said to Mary, the baby’s mother, “This child is destined to cause **many in Israel to fall**, and **many others to rise**. He has been sent as a sign from God, but **many will oppose him**. As a result, the deepest thoughts of many hearts will be revealed. And a sword will pierce your very soul.”
  — Luke 2:34-35

###### Jesus divides people
You would think the Messiah to come to unite us to God's presence. And yes, that is true. But the truth which Jesus spoke **promptly demands you to pick which side are you on**. Are you for Jesus or against Him?

That's what it means by *"cause many in Israel to fall, and many others to rise"*, and that *"many will oppose him"*.

His law is the revelator of the desires of many.
His blood washes away every iniquity and sin.
His death tore the veil from shame to righteousness.
His truth is like a sword that divides the stone into two halves.

### Jesus speaks with the teachers of Jerusalem

The leaders of Jerusalem are amazed at a twelve-year-old boy at His understanding and His answers.

When Jesus' parents found out that boy Jesus was not in their arms, they hurried back to Jerusalem and frantically searching for Him everywhere.

> 'His parents didn’t know what to think. “Son,” his mother said to him, “why have you done this to us? Your father and I have been frantic, searching for you everywhere.” '  
   —Luke 2:48

> ' “But why did you need to search?” he asked. “**Didn’t you know** that I must be in my Father’s house?” ' 
> — Luke 2:49

Jesus did not deliberately sin in front of His mother. Because He indicated that the parents forgot something that they discussed beforehand ***"Didn’t you know that I must be in my Father’s house?"***

His parents should already know that He is called to be with His Father's presence.
Even Jesus choose to dwell with the Father's presence. We must do the same thing.

Jesus did not say that you should disobey your parents. He prioritized first His relationship with the Father.

###### What did Jesus do after?
> 'Then he returned to Nazareth with them and **was obedient to them**. And his mother stored all these things in her heart. Jesus grew in wisdom and in stature and in favor with God and all the people.'  — Luke 2:51-52

Jesus remained obedient before and after when He was found by His parents. In the end, the Lord the anointed King chose to be obedient to His parents. Even though He acknowledges Himself to be the Almighty, **he honors** the people who were with Him and feeding Him and nurturing Him and protecting Him from the beginning.

Sometimes we get glued to the call or ministry that we forget that we have violated the very commandment and the very valuable commodity in the Kingdom economy: honor, especially honoring your parents.
