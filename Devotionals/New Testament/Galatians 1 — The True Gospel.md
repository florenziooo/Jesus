As Paul is about to start his early ministry, he wanted to send his letter to the Galatian churches addressing the distortion of the gospel. But he wanted to prove his apostolic authority first and he wanted to be respected of his office.

He said that he is not appointed by any human being but by the direct revelation of Jesus Christ and the divine commission provided by both the Father and the Son—later historically confirmed by the pillars of the church and the Jerusalem leadership.

### CENTRAL SCRIPTURE

> *'I am shocked that you are turning away so soon from God, who called you to himself through the loving mercy of Christ. **You are following a different way that pretends to be the Good News but is not the Good News at all. You are being fooled by those who deliberately twist the truth concerning Christ**. ' — Galatians 1:6-7*

Paul warned the churches that they might be hearing a different kind of "gospel".

- The Legalistic Gospel
	- The idea of having faith in Jesus Christ is not enough and that you practice certain religious rituals to obtain salvation.
- The Prosperity Gospel
	- The idea of believing the guaranty of God's promises, wealth and prosperity as core message for own personal benefit rather than actually addressing the sinful nature of a human being.
- The Activism Gospel
	- The belief of the Messiah coming down on earth to save the Jews from the political oppression of the Roman empire; highlighting Jesus as a revolutionary or a hero—not a spiritual Redeemer coming down to save humanity from sin (still a reality of today)
- The Antinomian Gospel (Grace without Lordship)
	- The belief of quickly receiving the Lord's forgiveness and grace but omitting disciplinary instructions from the King.

And others. Paul lets anyone be accursed for preaching a different kind of gospel they preach. This brings me to the question:
### What exactly is the true Gospel?

**Gospel means Good News.**
##### Analogy
You chose to go to the deeper waters because you wanted risky fun.
When you start to feel that you're slowly sinking, you panic and to solve it what *you think* is right—flapping your arms back and forth.
But the problem is: what you *think* is right—but it's not. Because you think you know better. And you realize that it's not working.
And the problem becomes worse than before.
And you are on your way to your demise.
But *luckily* here comes the lifeguard.
The lifeguard came to save you from the problem *you* created.
You admitted that you are in need of help.
Help came. And salvation happened instantly. He brought you back to the shore.
And you start listening to him and do exactly what he says.
Not because you do it to get saved, but because you wanted to be healed and be better moving forward in this lifetime.

##### The True Gospel
***Genesis 3:14-19 -> John 3:16 -> 1 Corinthians 15:1-4***

Heaven and Earth were once one.
God created humanity with the gift of free will.
Humans can choose to love or not love Him.
Everything was perfect.
Until humanity disobeyed the Lord.

The fall of humanity passed over from generation to generation.
And punishment came season after season.
**And so the God of the Most High who owned the universe...**
**Stepped down from His heavenly throne.**
**And He chose to be born in a little manger.**
And spent 33 years of His life full of joy and suffering.

He identified himself with the sinners with joy and compassion.
He wanted to feel what it's like to be human.
He allowed himself to cry, be tempted, and feel insufferable pain.
Where those things in heaven are perfectly non-existent.

And in his lifetime, He chose to be holy.
He fulfilled over 300 prophecies.
A probability of 1 in 100 quadrillion.
Taught principles and wisdom.
Showed signs and wonders.
Pursued His true purpose in life—to save humanity from sin.
But the religious leaders did not like what He claims to be.

But as human as He is, it was not easy for Him.
When that night he broke down, shedding blood, sweat, and tears.
As human as He is, He wanted to back out.
But He chose to submit to the will of His father.

**Hung on the cross on the mountain of Golgotha.**
**Beard torn, head punctured, skin lacerated, side pierced.**
**Died with the weight of all sin pressed against Him.**
**The sinless Lamb became sin Himself.**
**Messiah laid, buried; all alone.**

**And on the third day.**
**The death has overcome.**
**By the Son of God that rose from the dead.**
**The veil was torn. His mercy reigns.
That those who truly believe in Him
Nothing can separate them from His love.**

This death and resurrection undeniably shows that anything that He has to say HAS TO BE TRUE—that when He said that whosoever believes in Him, shall not perish but obtain eternal life. Everything that the Old Testament and the Law has to be fulfilled. And everything that the New Testament that His overwhelming grace supersedes the Law and sin. And that while have our places secured in heaven, He called us to be holy and set apart here on earth for His kingdom. This is where we achieve our true purpose.
